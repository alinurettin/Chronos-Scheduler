## 1. Problem Statement & Market Landscape
High-throughput asynchronous background worker with cron scheduling, dead-letter recovery and retry backoff.
Existing tools in this domain suffer from high operational bloat, cloud subscription fees, and complex setup requirements.
Chronos-Scheduler fills this gap as a lightweight, zero-dependency, self-hosted solution.
